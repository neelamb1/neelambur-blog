A useful local classified ad helps someone decide whether to respond without guessing. Whether the post is for a used item, rental, service, job, event, or community need, it should be specific, current, and safe to act on.

Short does not have to mean vague. A few verified details are more useful than a long promotional message.

## Include the essential details

A clear ad should state:

- What is being offered or requested.
- The relevant category.
- General location or a nearby public landmark.
- Price, rent, pay range, or whether it is negotiable, when applicable.
- Condition, availability, and important restrictions.
- A public contact method intended for responses.
- The date the information was last checked.

Photos should show the actual item or place when possible. Do not use unrelated images, misleading claims, or someone else's contact details.

## Protect private information

An ad rarely needs an exact home address, identity document, bank detail, one-time password, or other sensitive information. Begin with general location context and share additional details only when they are necessary and the other person has been reasonably verified.

Treat unexpected payment links, advance-fee demands, and urgent pressure carefully. A local-looking phone number or familiar place name does not prove that a person or offer is genuine.

## Keep the ad current

Classified information expires quickly. Mark an item sold, a vacancy filled, a rental unavailable, or an event completed. Repeatedly reposting old information wastes people's time and makes the directory harder to trust.

If important details change, update the original ad instead of creating several conflicting versions.

## What does not belong

Impersonation, prohibited goods, harassment, discriminatory language, false urgency, copied listings, and requests for unsafe payments do not make a useful community marketplace. Reports should identify the exact ad and the specific concern so it can be reviewed.

Good local classifieds work when readers can understand the offer, verify the important details, and recognize when it is no longer active.
